Last Light Avenue

The city's empty and the sun is going down at the end of the avenue. Hostiles come at you in waves, each bigger than the last, and the fight runs on into the night and through to the next day. Pick two guns, use the wrecks for cover, climb a fire escape for the high ground, and drop your night vision when the lights go out.
